Scientific work

Research

My research approach follows a connected pathway: understand the sensing mechanism, extract meaningful information from the signal, and translate it into a useful decision.

Advanced sensing materials

Functional materials and 2D systems for chemical and physical sensing, with emphasis on structure–property relationships and surface interactions.

Device physics and optoelectronics

Field-effect devices, heterostructures, photodetection and the relationship between device architecture and measured response.

AI-enabled monitoring

Data-driven analysis of complex sensor signals for detection, classification, interpretation and predictive monitoring.
